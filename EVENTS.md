# The Space Between: event context and vision

Draft for the delivery team. Where an answer isn't known yet it is marked to be defined and is on the to-do list. Everything here comes from `src/data/events.json` and the brief;
nothing is invented. 

**The shared purpose.** The Space Between is a community and audience development programme run by
Sonic Boom Music CIC, not a gig series. Events are how we bring people in. The point is a community
around live music in Burton, widening who comes (families, young people, people who wouldn't call
themselves music fans), and telling the story as it happens. People and their stories first, then
the music, then the logistics. A permanent venue is our ambition, not a plan: every event helps
build the evidence.

**For every event.** Book the venue(s), then artists, then technicians. Gather stories with
permission. Run the check-in or box-office count, then the survey within 48 hours.

Dates show as month only until tickets are on sale or a free event is confirmed.

---

## 1. Firelight: October 2026 (Fri 23 Oct, on sale)

- **Vision:** an intergenerational night of folk, song and stories with Burton Folk Club: music
  that's been passed down and songs still being written.
- **Who it's for:** Folk Club regulars, families and first-timers sharing songs and stories.
- **Confirmed:** Burton Library. Ticketed from £7.50 (early bird £6), evening, step-free.
- **Our job on the night:** gather Burton's music stories.
- **Status:** planning questions settled by the team.

## 2. Winter Sounds: December 2026

- **Vision:** the whole community together for winter, and the launch night for Founding
  Membership of The Space Between.
- **Who it's for:** everyone already in the community, plus the people who'll help make the case
  for what comes next.
- **Confirmed:** ticketed from £7.50, evening. Venue to be confirmed.
- **Membership launch:** a moment within the event, then shared afterwards. The Ticket Tailor
  membership link will be ready.
- **Still to define:**
  1. What do we want people to do on the night (join, bring a friend, tell a story)?
  2. Who is the lead act, or is the line-up still open?

## 3. New Year Community Sing: January 2027

- **Vision:** voices together to start the year. No experience, no audition, no pressure.
- **Who it's for:** anyone who has never been to one of our events.
- **Confirmed:** free, daytime, hot drinks. Venue to be confirmed.
- **Singing leaders:** Leah Wilcox, Andy Crowe and Carl North.
- **Still to define:**
  1. Is it open walk-in, or do people register?
  2. What songs or styles do we expect: familiar favourites, folk, pop?
  3. Does the hot-drinks offer have a partner or a cost line?

## 4. Love Your Local: February 2027

- **Vision:** a hop between Burton's live music venues in one night: local artists, local pubs, one
  wristband. A reminder that the town already has a music scene worth keeping.
- **Who it's for:** people who back local musicians; you choose who plays.
- **Confirmed:** ticketed from £7.50, evening. Venues to be confirmed.
- **To be defined (also on the to-do list):**
  1. How many venues, and which kinds (pubs only, or other spaces)?
  2. How do people "choose who plays": a vote beforehand, or choosing on the night?
  3. How does the wristband work (one ticket for all venues, a limit per venue)?
  4. Who books and pays the artists: us or the venues?

## 5. Family Music Discovery Day: March 2027

- **Vision:** instruments to try, musicians to meet, music to make together. A free Saturday
  morning for families to find out what live music feels like from the inside.
- **Who it's for:** families and children, with TURBO and Burton & South Derbyshire College
  students (instrument "petting zoo", taster workshops).
- **Confirmed:** free, daytime. Venue to be confirmed.
- **To be defined (also on the to-do list):**
  1. What exactly do TURBO and the College students each bring (instruments, tutors, volunteers)?
  2. What age range are we aiming at?
  3. Do we need safeguarding and DBS arrangements beyond the standard policy?
  4. Is there a baby-changing or quiet-space requirement we should put on the venue brief?

## 6. Global Sounds: April 2027

- **Vision:** music from across the world, made in and around Burton. A celebration of the town's
  cultures and the musicians who carry them.
- **Who it's for:** Burton's many communities, with music chosen by the communities it belongs to.
- **Confirmed:** ticketed from £7.50, evening. Co-curated with community ambassadors and Burton
  Caribbean Association. Venue to be confirmed.
- **To be defined (also on the to-do list):**
  1. Who are the community ambassadors, and how are they chosen and supported?
  2. Which cultures do we expect to represent, and who decides?
  3. What does co-curating mean in practice: the ambassadors choose the artists, or advise?
  4. How do we pay people for their time as ambassadors?

## 7. Music in the Market: May 2027

- **Vision:** live music in the heart of Burton's market: free, outdoors, impossible to miss.
- **Who it's for:** shoppers, families and passers-by who meet local music where they already are.
- **Confirmed:** free, daytime pop-up sets. Venue to be confirmed.
- **To be defined (also on the to-do list):**
  1. Is the market place the plan, and who do we need permission from (council, market operator)?
  2. How many sets, how long, and what is the weather fallback?
  3. How do we gather stories and sign-ups from people who didn't plan to come?

## 8. Summer Park Sessions: June 2027

- **Vision:** a long afternoon in the park with local artists across multiple stages. Bring a
  blanket, bring the kids.
- **Who it's for:** everyone, and the start of our summer Loyalty Passport.
- **Confirmed:** free, daytime, outdoor. Venue to be confirmed.
- **To be defined (also on the to-do list):**
  1. How does the Loyalty Passport work, and what do people get for coming?
  2. How many stages, and how many artists (this drives the technician and licensing plan)?
  3. What is the rain plan?
  4. Who looks after welfare and first aid at an outdoor event?

## 9. Youth Takeover: July 2027

- **Vision:** the stage belongs to Burton's young musicians. A night programmed by and for young
  people.
- **Who it's for:** young people and the next generation of Burton's music scene.
- **Confirmed:** free, evening, run by our Youth Programming Panel. Venue to be confirmed.
- **To be defined (also on the to-do list):**
  1. Who is on the Youth Programming Panel, and how were they recruited?
  2. What age range is the panel and the audience?
  3. What decisions do young people make themselves, and what stays with the team?
  4. Safeguarding: who is responsible for under-18s on the night?

## 10. Community Music Trail: September 2027

- **Vision:** the year ends and the case is made. A trail of music across Burton's spaces,
  charities and community venues, one month before the next Sonic Boom Festival.
- **Who it's for:** the whole year's community, passport in hand.
- **Confirmed:** free, daytime. Venues to be confirmed.
- **To be defined (also on the to-do list):**
  1. Which kinds of spaces and charities do we hope to include?
  2. What does the passport collect, and what is the finale or reward?
  3. How do we show "the case is made": a summary of the year, a public moment, a report?
  4. Is the date firm against the festival?
